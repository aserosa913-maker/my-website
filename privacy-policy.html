Privacy Policy

This page explains how the independent operator of this website handles information when you visit the site or contact us.

Effective date: October 25, 2026

1. Who operates this website

This website is an independent informational guide for common food-delivery questions. It is not owned or operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by DoorDash. The phone number displayed on this site is for the independent site operator and is not DoorDash's official customer-service number.

2. Information you may provide

You can read the guides without creating an account. If you call or otherwise contact the site operator, you may choose to share information such as your name, phone number, and details of your question. Please do not share passwords, one-time verification codes, full payment-card numbers, or other sensitive account credentials.

3. Information collected automatically

When you visit a website, hosting and security systems may process technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, timestamps, and diagnostic logs. Such information may be used to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the site. The exact information retained depends on the hosting and security services in use.

4. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Your browser or third-party services used to host, measure, or advertise this website may use cookies or similar technologies. If analytics or advertising measurement tools are enabled, they may process information about visits or ad interactions under their own policies and settings. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some site functions or measurement.

5. How information may be used

  • To respond to a call or message you initiate.
  • To maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the website.
  • To understand general site usage and improve the guides, where analytics tools are enabled.
  • To measure advertising performance, where advertising measurement tools are enabled.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements and help prevent abuse.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information that you provide directly to the site operator. Information may be processed by service providers supporting hosting, security, analytics, or advertising when those services are used. This website also links to third-party websites, including DoorDash resources. Those websites have their own privacy policies and terms, which you should review before sharing information with them.

7. Data retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, to maintain relevant records, or to meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards may be used to protect information, but no website, transmission, or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to provide personal information when browsing the guides. If you have shared information with the site operator and want to ask about it or request correction or deletion, contact the operator using the number shown on the website, subject to applicable law and any records that must be retained.

9. Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the site operator, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions about this independent website, contact the site operator at 507-722-0465. This is not DoorDash's official customer-service number.

Important: For order-specific issues, payments, refunds, or DoorDash account changes, use the official DoorDash app or website. Do not share passwords or verification codes with this independent site.
