About this website

This website publishes general informational guides about food-delivery orders, payments, memberships, account access and common delivery questions. It is an independent guide and is not operated by DoorDash.

Information this website receives

This website does not provide a customer account, order lookup, payment processing or delivery-tracking service. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, verification codes, order details or other sensitive information through this website.

Like many websites, the hosting provider may process basic technical information when a page is requested, such as an IP address, browser type, device information and request time, for security, delivery and troubleshooting. The exact information retained depends on the hosting provider's configuration and policies.

Calling the assistance number

If you choose to call the phone number displayed on this website, your call is handled through your phone carrier and the recipient's telephone service. Your carrier may process call and billing information under its own privacy policy. Do not share passwords, one-time verification codes or full payment-card details over a call.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website may use essential browser or hosting technologies needed to display and operate its pages. If analytics, advertising or other optional tracking tools are added, this policy should be updated to describe those tools and the choices available to visitors.

Links to other websites

This website may link to external websites for additional information. Those websites have their own privacy practices and terms. Review their policies before sharing personal information with them.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website changes. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates when this page was most recently revised.
