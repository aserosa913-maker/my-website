Charge 3 setup & troubleshooting

Use this Charge 3-specific guide for setup, connection, syncing, charging and common troubleshooting questions.

Call +1 (507) 722-0465View all Fitbit modelsOfficial Fitbit Help

1. Set up your Charge 3

Install the Fitbit app, sign in, turn on Bluetooth, and follow the in-app steps to add Charge 3. Keep the device close to your phone during setup and make sure the Fitbit app has the permissions it requests.

2. If it will not connect

Keep the device close to your phone, confirm Bluetooth is on, check app permissions, and restart the phone and device before trying again.

3. If syncing is not working

Open the Fitbit app and try a manual sync. Check Bluetooth, internet access and available app or device updates.

4. Charging help

Use the charging accessory designed for this model and make sure the charging contacts are aligned. If the model uses a replaceable battery, use the correct battery type instead.

5. Notifications and account access

Check phone notification permissions and app settings. For account problems, use official account recovery options. Never share passwords or verification codes with someone who contacts you.
