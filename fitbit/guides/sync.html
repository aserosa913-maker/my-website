How to Fix Fitbit Syncing

If your Fitbit is not updating activity or health data in the app, check the basic connection and app conditions first.

Steps to try

  1. 1. Keep the Fitbit near your phone.
  2. 2. Confirm Bluetooth is enabled.
  3. 3. Open the Fitbit app and try a manual sync.
  4. 4. Check that the app has the required permissions.
  5. 5. Make sure your phone has internet access.
  6. 6. Update the Fitbit app and restart the phone and Fitbit if needed.
