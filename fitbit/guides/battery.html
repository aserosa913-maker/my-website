Battery care basics

General factors that influence wearable battery life.

Battery life depends on the model, battery age, settings, usage, and environment. Consult the instructions supplied for your specific device for supported charging methods.

Screen and display use

Frequent screen activation or high brightness can use more power on devices with these features.

Connected features

Notifications, wireless connections, and some background features may affect how often a device needs charging.

Temperature

Very hot or cold conditions can affect battery performance. Follow the manufacturer’s temperature and charging guidance.

Charging safety

Use compatible charging accessories and inspect cables for damage. Do not use a damaged battery or accessory; consult the manufacturer’s guidance if the device becomes unusually hot or swollen.
