Settings and personalization

Understand common preferences before changing them.

Settings let you adapt a wearable to your routine. Labels and menu locations vary by model and app version, so use the device’s own menus and documentation for exact instructions.

Notifications

Review which apps are permitted to send notifications to your wearable. Too many alerts can be distracting and may affect battery use.

Display preferences

Brightness, screen wake behavior, and screen timeout can affect readability and power consumption on models that offer these options.

Goals and reminders

Activity goals and reminders can help structure routines. They are optional and should be adjusted to suit your needs.

Permissions

Phone Bluetooth, notification, and background permissions may affect how connected features behave.
