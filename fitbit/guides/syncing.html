Bluetooth and syncing concepts

A simple overview of how connected data updates work.

Many wearable devices use Bluetooth to exchange information with a compatible phone. Successful syncing can depend on device compatibility, permissions, connectivity, and software versions.

Bluetooth connection

Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology. A phone and wearable generally need to be within range and have wireless connectivity enabled.

App permissions

Operating-system permissions and background activity settings can affect connected features. The relevant controls vary by phone.

Updates

Device firmware and companion apps may receive updates that change features or compatibility. Read update notes and follow the official instructions for your model.

Data timing

Some information may not appear instantly. Sync frequency and supported data types vary by device and app.
