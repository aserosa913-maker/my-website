Device features explained

A plain-language introduction to common wearable features.

Wearable devices can combine timekeeping, movement tracking, notifications, and wellness summaries. The exact features available depend on the device model, software, region, and account configuration.

Activity summaries

Many trackers estimate daily movement and present it in an app or on the device. These estimates are useful for observing patterns over time, but they are not perfect measurements.

Notifications

Some models can display selected phone notifications when compatible devices are connected and the relevant permissions are enabled.

Health-related sensors

Some devices include sensors that estimate metrics such as heart rate. Availability and measurement conditions differ by model; readings should not be treated as a medical diagnosis.

Personal goals

Goals can help make activity patterns easier to follow. Choose realistic targets and adjust them to your own circumstances.
