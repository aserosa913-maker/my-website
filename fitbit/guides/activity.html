Activity and wellness metrics

What common tracking summaries can—and cannot—tell you.

Wearable metrics are estimates intended to help people notice trends. Accuracy can vary with device fit, movement type, sensor design, and other factors.

Steps and distance

Step counts and distance estimates can be useful for comparing your own activity across days. They may be less accurate for some movements or when the device is not worn as intended.

Active minutes

Some devices estimate time spent in activity. The method and labels vary across models and software.

Heart-rate estimates

Optical sensors estimate heart rate from signals at the skin. Fit, movement, temperature, and other conditions can affect readings.

Sleep summaries

Sleep features estimate sleep periods and patterns; they do not replace clinical assessment. If you have health concerns, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
