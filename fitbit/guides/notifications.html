Fitbit Notification Troubleshooting

When calls, texts or app notifications do not appear, the issue may involve phone permissions, Bluetooth or Fitbit app settings.

Steps to try

  1. 1. Confirm the Fitbit is connected to the phone.
  2. 2. Check phone notification permissions.
  3. 3. Check notification settings in the Fitbit app.
  4. 4. Keep Bluetooth enabled.
  5. 5. Restart the phone and Fitbit and test again.
