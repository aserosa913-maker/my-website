How to Set Up a Fitbit

For a new Fitbit, start with the Fitbit app and follow its setup flow. Keep your device close to your phone, enable requested permissions, and follow the model-specific instructions shown by the app.

Steps to try

  1. 1. Install or update the Fitbit app.
  2. 2. Sign in using the account associated with your Fitbit.
  3. 3. Turn on Bluetooth and keep the device close to your phone.
  4. 4. Follow the app's device setup instructions.
  5. 5. Allow the permissions needed for syncing and notifications.
  6. 6. If setup fails, restart the phone and Fitbit and try again.
