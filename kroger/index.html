Step-by-Step Guides

General troubleshooting for common grocery-order questions.

Missing or incorrect items

  1. Compare items received with your order receipt.
  2. Keep the order number and photos of damaged or incorrect items if useful.
  3. Report the issue using Kroger’s official website or app.

Delivery status or late order

  1. Check order status and the estimated delivery window in your account.
  2. Confirm the delivery address and review order notifications.
  3. If marked delivered but missing, contact Kroger through official help channels.

Refund and payment questions

  1. Review the order receipt and payment statement.
  2. Check the order details for refund or adjustment information.
  3. For account-specific billing decisions, use Kroger’s official website or app.

Account or app troubleshooting

  1. Confirm you are using the correct sign-in details.
  2. Update the app and use the official password-reset process if needed.
  3. Never share passwords or verification codes with this independent website.

Digital coupons and rewards

  1. Sign in to the correct loyalty account.
  2. Check coupon dates, terms, eligible products, and activation requirements.
  3. Keep your receipt and contact Kroger through official channels about missing points or discounts.

Pickup orders

  1. Review the selected pickup store and scheduled time.
  2. Check messages about substitutions, delays, or unavailable items.
  3. Use official account or store contact details for order-specific changes.

Boost membership

  1. Review membership status and renewal details in your official account.
  2. Check benefit eligibility and purchase requirements.
  3. Use official membership settings for billing or cancellation questions.

Change or cancel an order

  1. Open order details as soon as possible.
  2. Look for edit or cancellation options; availability depends on order status.
  3. If no option is available, contact Kroger through official help channels.
