ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

Clear information for common grocery-order questions.

Kroger Order & Tracking Help is an independent informational website that publishes general educational guides about delivery, pickup, order tracking, missing items, payments, coupons, and account access.

This website is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by The Kroger Co. or its brands. It cannot access customer accounts, retrieve private order details, change orders, or issue refunds.

What the guides do

The articles explain common processes in plain language and help readers identify where to find the appropriate official information.

Limitations

Retailer policies and app features can change. Confirm order-specific information directly through the retailer’s official website or app.

Contact

For questions about the content of this website, call 507-722-0465. This is the independent website operator’s contact number, not Kroger customer service.
