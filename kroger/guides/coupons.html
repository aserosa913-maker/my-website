🏷️ Coupons and offers

This guide shares general information about common grocery-order questions. Available options depend on the retailer, order status, location, and current policy.

Offer availability and terms can change.

Steps to consider

  1. Read the offer’s terms, eligible items, and expiration date.
  2. Check whether activation is required in the official app or account.
  3. Confirm minimum purchase, location, or membership requirements.
  4. Review the final cart total before placing an order.
  5. Contact the retailer through official channels if an offer does not apply.

For account-specific questions

For order-specific questions, use Kroger’s official website or app. This site provides general information only.
