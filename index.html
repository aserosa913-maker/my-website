Helpful Step-by-Step Guides

General information for common delivery issues.

GUIDE 01

Missing or incorrect order

  1. Open the official app and select your order.
  2. Choose the order-help or report-a-problem option.
  3. Select the issue and describe it accurately.
  4. Follow the resolution options shown.
GUIDE 02

Order not delivered

  1. Check the delivery photo and drop-off notes.
  2. Look around your designated drop-off area.
  3. Confirm the address in your order details.
  4. Report the issue through official order help.
GUIDE 03

Late delivery

  1. Check the latest arrival estimate in the app.
  2. Review delivery updates or messages.
  3. Open order help if the delay is substantial.
  4. Use official support for order-specific help.
GUIDE 04

Refund request

  1. Find your order and any refund confirmation.
  2. Check the original payment method.
  3. Review the platform's processing timeline.
  4. Contact official support for an update.
GUIDE 05

Payments and charges

  1. Compare the transaction with your receipt.
  2. Check whether it may be a pending authorization.
  3. Never share passwords or one-time codes.
  4. For unfamiliar charges, contact your bank and the platform.
GUIDE 06

Promo codes and credits

  1. Check the code's expiry and eligibility terms.
  2. Confirm the order meets any minimum requirement.
  3. Enter the code before checkout.
  4. Ask official support about eligible codes that fail.
GUIDE 07

DashPass membership

  1. Open membership settings in the official app.
  2. Review your plan, renewal date and charges.
  3. Use the displayed cancellation process if needed.
  4. Save confirmation for your records.
GUIDE 08

Cancel an order

  1. Open the active order promptly.
  2. Check whether a cancellation option is available.
  3. Review any fee or refund notice before confirming.
  4. Use official help if you need assistance.
GUIDE 09

Account access

  1. Use the official account recovery process.
  2. Reset your password through the platform.
  3. Secure the email linked to your profile.
  4. Report suspicious activity through official channels.
GUIDE 10

App issues

  1. Check your connection and app updates.
  2. Close and reopen the app.
  3. Write down any error message.
  4. Contact official support if the issue continues.
GUIDE 11

Delivery details

  1. Review the address and delivery notes.
  2. Use in-app contact options where available.
  3. Do not post access codes publicly.
  4. Follow the app's options for active orders.
GUIDE 12

Restaurant issues

  1. Keep the receipt and packaging where practical.
  2. Take photos if the platform requests them.
  3. Report the issue against the correct order.
  4. Follow the resolution options from official support.

Frequently Asked Questions

What to know before contacting support.

Is this the official DoorDash website?

No. This is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by DoorDash.

Can this website issue refunds?

No. Refund eligibility and processing are handled by the delivery platform. Use its official app or Help Center for order-specific requests.

Does the phone number belong to DoorDash?

No. The displayed number is for the independent website operator, not DoorDash's official customer-service number.
