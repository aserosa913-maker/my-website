Frequently asked questions

Can this guide show my live order status?

No. Sign in to Kroger.com and check your order history for live tracking.

What if an item is missing or damaged?

Open the order details on Kroger.com and look for the available help or issue-reporting options.

Where can I check a refund?

Start from the order history in your Kroger account or contact Kroger through its official help center.

Should I enter my password or card details here?

No. This guide never asks for passwords, full payment-card details, or verification codes.
