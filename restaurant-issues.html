Restaurant Issues Guide

What to do about food quality, incorrect items, or damaged packaging.

Follow these steps

01

Keep the order information

Save the receipt and note which items were affected.

02

Document the issue

Take clear photos if the app asks for supporting details.

03

Select the relevant problem

Choose the food quality, incorrect item, or damaged order option that best matches.

04

Submit an accurate report

Follow the resolution options shown for the order.

Need help navigating these steps?

Call our site assistance line for help using this guide.
